# Authority outreach kit — Lake Geneva

The one thing no code change can do. Bing's own dashboard shows the ceiling:
113 pages indexed cleanly, 617 impressions in six months, backlinks "–".
The content ranks where authority isn't required (DuckDuckGo/Bing family) and
is absent where it is (Google). Links from real local institutions are the
only lever left, and they cannot be automated — which is why this is a kit to
send, not a function to deploy.

Three emails. Each is under 150 words because the person reading it is busy,
each asks for exactly one thing, and each leads with what we already do for
them — not with what we want. Send from a real name. All three institutions
below are real and already appear in this codebase's data pipeline or guides.

**Rules, non-negotiable:**
- Never mention reciprocal links. Never offer anything in exchange. One
  follow-up maximum, two weeks later, then drop it.
- Send individually. No BCC blasts, no mail-merge smell.
- These are relationships in a town of 8,000. The email is the first
  impression of the brand.

---

## 1. Lake Geneva Public Library

*Why they're first: libraries maintain community-resource link lists as a
core service, they are the most receptive linkers in any small town, and we
already syndicate their events calendar (`sync-library-events` runs daily) —
so the relationship already exists in one direction.*

> **Subject: Lake Geneva Brief — we list your events daily**
>
> Hi —
>
> I run Lake Geneva Brief (lakegenevabrief.com), a free daily news site for
> the Lake Geneva area. We pull the library's public events calendar into our
> events listings every day, so programs at the library reach readers who
> don't check the library site directly.
>
> If the library keeps a list of local news or community information
> resources, we'd be glad to be considered for it. The site is free, has no
> paywall, and covers city hall, schools, public safety, and community
> events.
>
> Either way, thank you for the events feed — it's one of the most-read
> parts of the site.
>
> [name]
> Lake Geneva Brief

## 2. Geneva Lake West Chamber of Commerce

*Why: chamber membership is a legitimate business membership that typically
includes a member-directory listing with a link. That is not a link scheme —
it's joining the chamber. The repo already syncs their public events twice
daily (`sync-geneva-lake-west-chamber-*`), same warranted-intro shape as the
library. The Lake Geneva Area Chamber (VISIT Lake Geneva) is the other one;
same letter, adjusted.*

> way for us to be part of the business community we write about.
>
> Could you send membership information?
>
> [name]

## 3. Geneva Lake Museum / Walworth County Historical Society

*Why: historical societies link to accurate local-history resources, and the
site's Shore Path and Streblow guides are careful history — every hedged
claim marked as such, sources named. The ask here is review, not a link: a
historian who corrects you becomes invested, and institutions link to things
they've helped make accurate.*

> **Subject: Our Shore Path history guide — would you check it?**
>
> Hi —
>
> I run Lake Geneva Brief, a local news site. We publish a detailed guide to
> the Geneva Lake Shore Path — its history, the public-access easement, and
> every public landmark along it — plus a history of Streblow boats on the
> lake:
>
> lakegenevabrief.com/guides/lake-geneva-shore-path
> lakegenevabrief.com/guides/streblow-boats-geneva-lake
>
> We were careful to mark what's the traditional account versus what's
> documented, but you know this history better than anyone. If anything in
> either guide is wrong or incomplete, I'd genuinely like to correct it, with
> credit to the museum.
>
> [name]

## The realtor rule (for when agents ask — they will)

Realtors want to appear in the cost-of-living and neighborhoods guides and
will link to them. That's real, and it's also an implicit link exchange
unless governed by a public rule. If it isn't published, don't do it.

The rule, verbatim, to put on the guides that name businesses:

> Businesses and professionals appear in our guides by editorial judgment
> and public record only. Inclusion cannot be purchased, is never
> conditioned on anything, and we do not exchange or solicit links.

## What NOT to do — decided, not open

- No paid links, directory-submission runs, guest-post networks, or
  link exchanges. Detectable, worthless for a news brand, fatal at fleet
  scale.
- No mass outreach automation. One person sending 1,000 templated emails is
  spam with extra steps; the three above work because they're individually
  true.
- No pitching regional press for coverage "about the site." A story cited
  *by* the press comes from the incident/data record being genuinely useful,
  not from asking.

## Sequencing

Send all three in one afternoon. Expected honest yield: one to two links
and one relationship within a couple of months — which, on a domain whose
backlink column currently reads "–", is not a small thing. Google's
authority gate moves on exactly this kind of link: local, editorial,
unbought.

The GPX file (`/shore-path-geneva-lake.gpx`, linked from the Shore Path
guide) is the passive half of the same play: walking-forum and hiking-site
links accrue to downloadable tracks without outreach. If a walker's forum
ever asks, the answer is yes, free, attribution appreciated.
