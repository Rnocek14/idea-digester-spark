Tokens (in src/index.css, exposed via Tailwind):
--lake-light 200 80% 82%, --lake-blue 205 65% 68%, --lake-deep 205 55% 38%,
--lake-navy 215 70% 35%, --shore-terracotta 25 75% 42%, --lake-sand 45 30% 96%.
--gradient-lake = light to blue 135deg. Tailwind: lake-light|DEFAULT|deep|navy|sand, shore-terracotta.

Use as accents only: hero strips, section headers, single divider bars, icon fills,
newsletter masthead, Selling Lake Geneva hero. The Shore Path map is the signature use.

Never apply to story cards, civic alerts, incidents, the LLL feed, or dashboards.
Those stay neutral so red/amber alert states keep their urgency. Bathing the feed in
lake-blue dilutes the map's signature moment.
